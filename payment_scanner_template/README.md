When you open `payment_scanner_template.psd` in Photoshop, you will see the layers organized from top to bottom:

```text

├── 01. Text - Amount Notice Footer          [BE SURE TO CHECK THE AMOUNT BEFORE PAYING]
├── 02. Text - QR CODE Placeholder           ["QR CODE" centered text]
├── 03. [EDIT] Place Your QR Code Here       [Demo QR code with payment badge; delete & paste client's QR]
├── 04. QR Frame - Corner Brackets           [4 Rose-gold / blush corner focus brackets]
├── 05. QR Card - White Card Base            [Crisp white rounded rectangle]
├── 06. QR Card - Drop Shadow                [Soft Gaussian blurred elevation shadow]
├── 07. Text - FOR PAYMENT                   [Tracked clean sans-serif]
```

## 🔤 Recommended Free Fonts

To match the typography identically, use these free fonts (Google Fonts / Adobe Fonts):

1. **Title ("SCAN")**:
   - **Google Font**: [Playfair Display Bold](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display) (Free)
   - **Adobe / System**: *Bodoni 72 Bold* or *Didot Bold*
2. **Script Accent ("Here")**:
   - **Google Font**: [Caveat Bold](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Caveat) or [Great Vibes](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Great+Vibes) (Free)
   - **Adobe / System**: *Snell Roundhand* or *Amsterdam / Holland Script*
3. **Subtitles & Footers ("FOR PAYMENT", "BE SURE TO CHECK...")**:
   - **Google Font**: [Montserrat Medium / SemiBold](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Montserrat) (Free, all caps with letter-spacing / tracking set to `200–300`)
   - **Adobe / System**: *Futura Medium* or *Avenir Next*

## 🛠️ How to Edit in Photoshop (Quick Step-by-Step)

1. **Open the PSD file**:
   - Open `payment_scanner_template.psd` in **Adobe Photoshop** (or [Photopea.com](https://www.photopea.com) if you do not have Photoshop installed).
2. **Add Your Client's QR Code**:
   - Locate layer `03. [EDIT] Place Your QR Code Here`.
   - Hide or delete this demo layer, then drag and drop your client's UPI / Paytm / PhonePe / GPay / Stripe / PayPal QR code onto the canvas.
   - Scale it to fit neatly inside the 4 pink corner brackets (around 360 × 360 px to 420 × 420 px).
3. **Add Your Client's Logo / Name**:
   - Select layer `11. [EDIT] Your Logo / Business Name` at the top.
   - Replace with your client's logo mark or type their store name (e.g. *The Bloom Cafe*, *Luxe Salon*, *Organic Superstore*).
4. **Customize or Translate Text**:
   - Select the **Horizontal Type Tool (T)** to change headings, currency notes, or add custom instructions.
5. **Export for Printing**:
   - Go to **File → Export → Export As...** or **Save As...**
   - Choose **PDF (Press Quality)** or **PNG (300 DPI)** for high-resolution acrylic printing!
